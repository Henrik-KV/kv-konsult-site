# Mobile composition

Mobile is not the desktop grid stacked vertically. Direction D is used here only as the current recommendation.

## First screen

- White header, supplied logo, company name and one clear contact action.
- Do not reduce the header to a tiny logo plus a default hamburger if three primary destinations can fit an accessible menu treatment.
- Use a shorter natural heading than the concept prototype.
- Keep verified Lägesbild status and the top of a real screen within the first visual sequence.
- Avoid a category eyebrow above the heading.

## Content order

1. What KV does and who it helps.
2. Immediate proof: Lägesbild.
3. How to choose an entry point.
4. One useful service explanation or illustrative scenario.
5. Additional proof and accurately labelled MÄSSY demo.
6. People and direct contact.
7. Low-commitment next step.

This order differs from desktop by moving proof upward and shortening transitional copy.

## Typography

- Target 42–52 px for the main mobile heading, adjusted by actual Swedish line breaks.
- Keep paragraph width between roughly 28 and 36 characters when practical.
- Avoid orphaned prepositions and one-word final lines.
- Do not let a headline consume more than one full viewport without proof or explanation.

## Media

- Do not render phones as tiny floating mockups.
- Crop one screen large enough to read, with the second screen partially entering the next state.
- Reserve aspect ratio to avoid layout shift.
- MÄSSY screens remain secondary and clearly marked as demo.

## Motion

- No mobile pinning longer than one viewport.
- Use two or three short scroll states, not a desktop-length sticky story.
- With reduced motion, show the final readable arrangement immediately.
- Do not hide copy or links behind animation.

## Required reviews

- Section work: 390×844 desktop-paired review.
- Release: 320×568, 375×812, 390×844, 430×932, 768×1024 and landscape mobile.
- Inspect text wrapping, dead space, image crop, content order, touch targets, menu, forms, fixed elements and motion.

## Current concept weaknesses

- A: serif headline is long and formal.
- B: too much dead space before the first full product screen.
- C: the name block needs authentic photography to feel human rather than designed.
- D: proof should rise slightly and the heading needs one less line.

None is safe to show a client as a finished design.
