# Motion storyboard

Motion is not approved until a visual direction is selected. This storyboard is for Direction D.

## Premise

Verified work becomes progressively easier to inspect inside a luminous field derived from the KV logo. The animation does not use abstract workflow nodes, fake dashboards or a generic chaos-to-order diagram.

## Desktop sequence

### State 1 — Immediate offer

- The page loads with complete readable copy and the top of two real Lägesbild screens.
- A faint K or V outline is visible behind the material.
- No entrance animation delays comprehension.

### State 2 — Light reveals detail

- During the first short scroll, a cyan light band passes across the real screens.
- The map screen moves from a partial crop to a readable product view.
- The label “Publicerad i App Store” remains stationary and factual.

### State 3 — Capabilities enter through the work

- The screen arrangement shifts aside and reveals a continuous service surface.
- Utbilda, Införa and Bygga appear at different scales according to the selected story, not as equal cards.
- If an illustrative project scenario is used, it is explicitly labelled as an example, not a client case.

### State 4 — Proof becomes dominant

- The luminous background fades toward white.
- Three Lägesbild screens become large enough to inspect.
- MÄSSY enters later and remains labelled “Demo · inte ett kundcase”.

### State 5 — Human handoff

- Product motion stops before the contact section.
- The final CTA is static, fast and fully usable.

## Mobile sequence

- State 1 includes copy, verified status and a visible product crop.
- One short scroll separates the two product screens; no long sticky hold.
- The service surface follows directly, with a different order if needed.
- Proof uses one large screen at a time.
- The CTA is static.

## Reduced motion and no JavaScript

- Show the final position of every object.
- All text, links, labels and proof remain in normal document flow.
- No essential content relies on opacity, transform or scroll progress.
- Do not pin the page or create artificial scroll distance.

## Performance limits

- Prefer CSS transforms and opacity on existing DOM or SVG masks.
- Use no video for the primary offer.
- Do not add an animation dependency before confirming the existing stack cannot deliver the chosen sequence.
- Clean up observers and listeners.

## Rejection risks

- Glow becomes generic technology decoration.
- Letterform becomes a meaningless watermark.
- Screens move but do not become easier to understand.
- Mobile inherits desktop timing.
- The sequence is described as a case despite lacking verified client evidence.
