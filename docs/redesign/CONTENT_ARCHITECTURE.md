# Content architecture

## Position

KV Konsult helps Swedish companies, municipalities, and organisations turn AI and digital opportunities into practical working methods and useful solutions.

## Delivery model

1. **Educate** — shared understanding, practical training, leadership sessions, and customised workshops.
2. **Implement** — readiness, priorities, governance, workflow redesign, adoption, and follow-up.
3. **Build** — web/mobile applications, internal tools, websites, integrations, and selected digital communication support.

## Homepage narrative

Header → “AI som går att använda på måndag” → verified work signal → Educate/Implement/Build → scroll story from fragmented workday to working method → distinct municipality/company paths → Lägesbild and MÄSSY evidence → named team/direct contacts → specific first-conversation CTA.

## Copy rules

- Use concrete Swedish working situations: meeting notes, delegations, reports, case handling, customer requests, Microsoft 365, and internal tools.
- State delivery inputs, activities, outputs, customer preparation, and next step on every major service page.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives, anonymous quantified outcomes, invented customers, and pricing.
- Use “ni” consistently for organisational buyers.
