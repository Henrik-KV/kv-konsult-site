# Final self-audit

## Verdict

The first warm editorial direction was rejected during review and was not retained. The implemented direction returns to KV's recognisable light-blue energy on a white base, removes the old template's stock imagery and fake proof, and adds a specific workflow story. It is materially stronger in structure, evidence, accessibility, and conversion.

This is not an unconditional visual approval. The homepage hero, sticky workflow, and product cases are the strongest parts. The team section remains visually thin because verified portraits, roles, and biographies do not exist. The site should be reviewed by the owner on the real Preview before any production decision.

## Anti-template review

- No stock-office hero, AI brain, robot, particle wallpaper, anonymous testimonial, fake counter, or generic dashboard mockup.
- Hero and scroll story use a bespoke document-to-workflow system tied to the value proposition.
- Utbilda, Införa, and Bygga share one connected process surface rather than three unrelated service cards.
- Lägesbild is shown as a released product; MÄSSY is explicitly a demo.
- Audience copy differs by operating context instead of swapping labels on the same paragraph.
- Contact, team, and proof use only verified names, channels, and assets.

## Remaining weaknesses

1. Authentic team/workshop photography is missing; the site cannot show the people with the same visual strength as the product work.
2. Individual team roles and biographies still require owner-approved facts.
3. NearYou relationship wording, named customer work, testimonials, and quantified outcomes remain unpublished pending verification.
4. The dependency audit still reports inherited findings; no automatic breaking upgrade was applied during the redesign.
