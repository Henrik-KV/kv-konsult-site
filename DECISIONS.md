# Decisions

## Rejected

- Current public Preview: technically functional, visually rejected, not client-safe.
- White/electric-blue as a sterile pseudo-interface system.
- Beige, brown, burgundy, orange, sand, or warm editorial palettes.
- Numbered navigation and unnecessary sequence numbers.
- Arrows appended to ordinary internal links.
- Pseudo-console labels, fake status language, dot-separated eyebrows, and design-system showcase captions.
- Abstract workflow diagrams as the main identity.
- Repeated slogan fragments and three-part copy formulas.
- Three equal service cards for Utbilda, Införa, Bygga.
- Mobile that simply stacks desktop modules.

## Locked palette

- White is the dominant field.
- Living light blue and luminous cyan provide energy.
- Deep KV blue and dark blue text provide contrast.
- The logo gradient is valid brand equity; use it deliberately, not as a reason for generic tech glow.

## Pending selection

- Selected direction: none.
- Recommendation for review: Direction D, “Luminöst arbete”.
- Typography: pending Gate 2; serif is only a comparison variable, not an approved system.
- Image strategy: verified interfaces first; authentic photography when supplied and approved.
- Motion strategy: actual work moves through a luminous field; no node diagram or fake dashboard.
- Mobile strategy: proof remains in the first screen sequence and choreography is recomposed for mobile.

## Tooling

- Claude Design was not available in the current toolset. No paid tool, asset, font, credit, or subscription was used.
